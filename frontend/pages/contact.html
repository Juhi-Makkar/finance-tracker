Frequently Asked Questions

Is FinTrack free to use?
Yes! FinTrack is completely free. All features including analytics, budgets, goals and premium themes are available at no cost.
Is my financial data secure?
Absolutely. We use JWT authentication and MongoDB Atlas with industry-standard encryption. Your data is stored securely in the cloud.
Can I export my data?
Yes! You can export your transactions as CSV or PDF from the Reports page or the Settings page anytime.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes, FinTrack is fully responsive and works beautifully on phones, tablets and desktops.
Can I change the currency?
Yes! FinTrack supports ₹ INR, $ USD, € EUR and £ GBP. You can change it anytime from your Profile or Settings page.
